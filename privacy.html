Privacy notice

Draft for the firm to review. The current website says a privacy policy is being updated; this is a plain-English starting point under the UK GDPR.

Who we are

Leung & Co Law is a trading name of Austen-Jones Solicitors Ltd (company no. 07526477), 145 Upland Road, London SE22 0DF. For notarial matters, the data controller is Amelia Leung, Notary Public.

What we collect and why

Your name, contact details, identity documents and the information you give us about your matter. We use it to advise and act for you, to meet our legal and regulatory duties (including anti-money-laundering checks), and to keep proper records.

Who we share it with

Only those who need it for your matter: courts and tribunals, the Home Office, HM Land Registry, lenders, other parties' solicitors, our regulators and insurers, and service providers bound by confidentiality. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

Usually at least six years after your matter ends, longer for wills, probate and property files.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Contact info@leung-solicitors.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

This website

This concept website uses no cookies or analytics. The enquiry form opens your own email app and stores nothing.
