Leung & Co Law has joined Austen-Jones Solicitors (SRA 557238). Our service to clients in the UK and abroad has not changed: Amelia Leung continues to act for you, with more support behind her.

What we do

A general practice with an emphasis on immigration, commercial and residential property, for clients in Bristol, elsewhere in the UK and overseas.

All practice areas →

  • Immigration and nationality

    Preparing and submitting immigration applications, and advice and representation at the First-tier Tribunal (Immigration and Asylum Chamber) on appeals against Home Office decisions. Business immigration, dependants and family reunion.

  • Residential conveyancing

    Freehold or leasehold sales and purchases, mortgages and remortgages.

  • Small business and commercial property

    Commercial contracts, buying and selling a business, and leases of commercial premises. This is the firm's specialism.

  • Wills and probate

    Non-contentious probate and estate administration where the assets are in the UK, and re-sealing of foreign grants of probate.

  • Licensing

    Advice, assistance and representation on licensing applications for business premises, including food safety.

  • Notarial services

    Amelia Leung is a Notary Public. Documents for use abroad, e-notarisation with e-apostille, and home or workplace visits.
