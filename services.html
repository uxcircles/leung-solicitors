Immigration and nationality

  • We prepare and submit immigration applications for individuals.
  • We advise on and represent clients in appeals at the First-tier Tribunal (Immigration and Asylum Chamber) against Home Office visa or immigration decisions.
  • Business immigration under the points-based system: investors, the self-employed, study and work in the UK.
  • Dependants and family reunion.
  • Member of the Immigration Law Practitioners' Association.
