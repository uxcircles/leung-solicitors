Established in November 1996 by Amelia Leung

Leung & Co undertakes a wide range of work, including property and conveyancing, business affairs, immigration and nationality, debt and licensing. Our clients are based in Bristol and beyond, in the UK and overseas, and come from many backgrounds.

Amelia Leung

Principal solicitor and Notary Public

Amelia studied for her first degree, in Social Science, at the Chinese University of Hong Kong. She completed her law studies in Newcastle upon Tyne, followed by a two-year training contract with a firm of solicitors, and qualified in 1992.

She was admitted as a solicitor in Hong Kong in 1998 and later in Ireland, and became a Notary Public in 2010 after the Postgraduate Diploma in Notarial Practice at the University of Cambridge.

Over the years she has developed expertise in business immigration under the points-based system, and in immigration work for dependants and family reunion. The immigration team has acted for clients from 20 different countries.
